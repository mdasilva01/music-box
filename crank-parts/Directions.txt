Directions for Constructing Your Drawing Machine

1. 3D print all of the pieces in the original dimension or 
   rescale everything (including the cam) as one.

2. Once printed and supports are removed, slip the slider 
   mechanism and stand into place on the side opposite the 
   cam-holder.

3. Lock the cam into place. The follower has a flat face that 
   can fit in the groove and be turned to prevent it from exiting.

4. Place the follower in the slider mechanism and attach the pen 
   holder on the other side. Put your desired writing utensil in 
   the slot.

5. Place a piece of paper on the stand to obtain your drawing!
